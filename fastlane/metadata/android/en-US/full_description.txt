QuickSetTiles enables you to create and customize Android Quick Settings tiles.

Key Features:
- Apps & Shortcuts: Launch any installed application or deep app shortcut directly from the Quick Settings panel.
- Split Screen: Pair two apps and launch them side-by-side in dual split-screen mode without accessibility services.
- Contacts: Direct call or SMS conversation tiles with SIM chooser.
- Shell Commands: Execute root (su) or Shizuku privileged shell commands with output popups.
- Collections: Group multiple tiles into a compact popup launcher tray.
- Websites & Files: Fast access to bookmarks, folders, and files.
- Lock Screen Privacy: Automatically hide tiles while the device is locked.

QuickSetTiles is free and open-source (GPLv3), contains zero analytics or trackers, and displays no advertisements.
